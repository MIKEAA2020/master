New theories, not just new theorems. The record-thermodynamic formalism: state, potentials, laws, phases, dynamics, and a measurement protocol for the bounded observer — held to its three first experimental confrontations. The record-phase p-scan localizes the first boundary at 0.233810 exactly; the rebuilt three-replica chain walks the second boundary to 0.305; the E. coli cycle scan, resolved by layer, separates the epsilon-laws from the k-laws and certifies 0.361 per cycle as the cascade’s slowest case; and the optic-Nehari lemma is refuted exactly, repaired stably, and bridged to the small-gain law that the whole programme converges on.
